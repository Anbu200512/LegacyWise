Keep your plan current

When might it be time to review?

A change in life circumstances can be a useful prompt to revisit your plans and ask whether anything needs attention.

Even when life feels steady, you may want to check that your documents still reflect your wishes and that the people named to act on your behalf are still appropriate. Beneficiary designations and other account records may also need separate attention.

There is no single review schedule that fits everyone. Make a note of questions as they come up, and ask a qualified professional about timing and any local requirements that apply to your circumstances.

A helpful next step

Turn a life change into a plan review.

When something changes, take a moment to note what may need a second look. These simple steps can help you prepare for a conversation with a qualified professional.

Write down what changed

Note the life event and any new priorities, responsibilities, or questions it brings up.

Gather relevant documents

Collect the current documents or records you may want to discuss, and keep them somewhere secure.

Ask what should be reviewed

A qualified professional can help explain which updates may matter for your circumstances and local requirements.
