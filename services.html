Estate Document Review

Check whether your existing documents still reflect your wishes and spot questions to raise with a qualified legal professional.

A review may cover

  • Named executors, trustees, and beneficiaries
  • Changes in family circumstances or assets
  • Related documents and beneficiary designations

Legal advice and document updates should be handled by an appropriately qualified professional.

Our Approach

A measured process helps keep conversations focused on your priorities and next steps.

1. Initial Consultation

Discuss your general goals and concerns.

2. Understanding the Client's General Goals

Clarify what matters most to you.

3. Reviewing Relevant Information

Identify key details to inform planning options.

4. Discussing Available Planning Options

Explain general approaches relevant to your needs.

5. Preparing or Reviewing Documents with Qualified Professionals

Work with appropriate expertise for document preparation/review.

6. Periodic Review When Circumstances Change

Keep your plan current as life evolves.

Process depends on services requested and applicable law.
