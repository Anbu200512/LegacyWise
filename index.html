Estate Planning Basics

Start with the essentials: the people, documents, and decisions that shape a thoughtful plan.

Will Drafting Overview

A will is a legal document that outlines how your assets should be distributed and who should handle your affairs after your passing. Careful drafting helps ensure your wishes are clearly documented.

Trust Planning Overview

Trusts can serve various purposes depending on individual circumstances. Understanding the general options and considerations can help determine if a trust may be suitable for your situation.

Asset & Beneficiary Planning

Identifying assets and thoughtfully selecting beneficiaries are important steps. Proper planning helps ensure your intentions are clear and minimizes potential complications.

Family & Dependent Considerations

Planning with dependents and family members in mind requires sensitivity and care. Considering their needs is a key part of comprehensive estate planning.

Why Planning Ahead Matters

Planning ahead gives you control, provides clarity for loved ones, and can help prevent unnecessary stress during difficult times. It's a thoughtful gift to those you care about.

Frequently Asked Questions Preview

A few helpful starting points. Visit our FAQ page for more answers.

What is estate planning?

Estate planning is the process of preparing for the management and distribution of your assets. It typically involves documents like wills and other arrangements to carry out your wishes.

Why might I need a will?

A will helps ensure your assets are distributed according to your wishes. Without one, distribution may follow default rules that may not reflect your preferences.

How often should estate documents be reviewed?

It's good practice to review documents after major life events (marriage, divorce, birth, death) or every few years as circumstances change.
